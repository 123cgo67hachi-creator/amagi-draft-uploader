About

Amagi Draft Uploader is a personal desktop tool used by a single creator to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts, using the TikTok Content Posting API (Upload to TikTok / video.upload). The creator then finishes editing and posts from the TikTok app. It is not offered to other users.
